<p class="lead">
  I started engineering at TMU online, from home, in a different time zone. I moved to Toronto over the winter
  holiday in December 2021.
</p>

My first job here was at Mr. Sub in Toronto, from January 2022 to April 2023: a sales associate and cashier, responsible for money handling, and entering inventory data in my free time. It taught me to talk with anyone, work quickly as part of a team, and be responsible for the cash.
